Privacy Policy

Ivelisse Designs · Dallas, TX · Last updated November 16, 2026

What we collect

When you order a personalized item at an Ivelisse Designs event, we collect the details you enter on our order form: the name or text to engrave, your item and style choices, and, only if you choose to provide it, your mobile phone number.

How we use it

We use your order details to make your item. If you provide a mobile number, we use it only to text you about that order: a confirmation with your place in line, a notice when your item is ready, and one thank-you message with a link to our website and an invitation to leave a review. We send no more than 3 texts per order.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with or sold to anyone. We use service providers (Twilio for sending texts and Google Firebase for storing orders) solely to run this service.

Your choices

Providing a phone number is optional and not required to place an order. Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help. To ask us to delete your information, email hello@stephivelisse.com.
