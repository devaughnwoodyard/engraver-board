Ivelisse Designs Order Texts

At Ivelisse Designs live engraving and calligraphy events, guests scan a QR code at the engraving table to open our order form at devaughnwoodyard.github.io/ivelisse-orders. The final step of that form, shown below, is where guests can choose to get text updates about their order. Adding a phone number is optional and is not required to place an order.

By adding your number, you agree to get up to 3 texts from Ivelisse Designs about this order: your place in line, when it's ready, and one thank-you with a review link. Message frequency: up to 3 messages per order. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

What happens next

Guests who add a number receive a confirmation text, for example: "Ivelisse Designs: Thanks, Maria! Your tumbler order is in and you're #4 in line. Track it here: https://devaughnwoodyard.github.io/ivelisse-orders/ Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Questions: hello@stephivelisse.com
